SurBox modifications: mono PCM conversion, 39 note slices, 35 Hz high-pass,
per-note gain normalization, small equal-temperament pitch correction based
on the lower reed fundamental, onset/end fades, and looping metadata.
Playback adds a 60 ms loop crossfade, envelopes, and airflow intensity.
The recording's paired/octave reeds and natural beating are retained.
No claim of separate physical reed-stop samples is made.
Derived audio is distributed under CC0 as well. No endorsement by the creator,

wood/3D visual assets are original SurBox implementation assets, not recordings.
