SurBox 1.0.0 — local engineering preview
Published 2026-10-27

Native macOS 14+ harmonium. Supplied binary: Apple Silicon / arm64.

- Fixed-pivot, interactive 3D bellows with twelve accordion pleats.
- 37 keyboard keys, computer-keyboard chords and mouse glissando.
- Stateful airflow, reserve, air assist, volume, mute and octave controls.
- Tonic selection, sargam labels, practice scales and Sa/Pa drones.
- 39 bundled acoustic note recordings from Donya Quick's CC0 source.
- Real MacBook lid-angle input when the compatible sensor is exposed.
- Explicit synthesized fallback and unsupported-lid messages.

IMPORTANT
This is a native app download, not an instrument running on Vercel or in a browser.
The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized or Developer ID signed.
A downloaded copy may require macOS's normal trusted-app approval flow.
Do not disable Gatekeeper or other system protections globally.

The 2020 M1 MacBook Air used for development exposes no continuous lid-angle
sensor. Its open/closed switch cannot measure flapping. Actual physical lid
tracking on compatible hardware remains unverified. Software integration was
tested with explicitly injected readings, not fabricated hardware output.

Verification: 16 deterministic groups; 53 primary native checks. Human listening,
physical sensor behavior, VoiceOver, device hot-plug and wider hardware acceptance
remain open. Details: verification.txt and lid-tracking.txt.
